# NVIDIA --- Warren Buffett Investor Memo

Verdict: OWN

## Screening results

NVIDIA passes all three Buffett checks in the printed SEC report. Return
on equity is at least the required 15% in each of the five reported
fiscal years. Owner earnings are positive in every reported year, and
owner earnings increase from the first reported year to the last.

The report calculates owner earnings as operating cash flow less capital
spending. The reported figures are positive throughout the period and
show substantial growth over the five fiscal years.

## Interpretation

The mechanical screen is favorable. Consistently high reported return on
equity, positive owner earnings, and growth in owner earnings suggest a
business that has generated strong profits and cash after capital
spending.

However, passing these checks does not establish that NVIDIA is
attractively valued or that its current growth and profitability will
persist. The screen does not independently assess valuation, customer
concentration, competitive threats, cyclicality, or the sustainability
of demand for accelerated computing.

## Limitations and missing information

The screen is based on five reported fiscal years and on the accounting
measures selected by this exercise. Owner earnings here are a simplified
calculation, not a complete estimate of normalized free cash flow. The
report should also be checked against the underlying filings before
relying on any extracted XBRL values.

## Next research needed

Review NVIDIA's valuation and cash-flow expectations, customer and
supplier concentration, competitive position, and the durability of
demand for its products. Assess whether current profitability can
persist through a less favorable industry cycle before making an actual
investment decision.
