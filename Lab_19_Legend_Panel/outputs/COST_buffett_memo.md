# Costco — Warren Buffett Investor Memo

Verdict: OWN

## Screening results

Costco passes each of the Buffett checks in the printed SEC report. Return on equity is above the screen's required level in every reported fiscal year. Owner earnings are positive in every reported fiscal year, and owner earnings increase from the first reported year to the last.

The report shows owner earnings calculated from operating cash flow less capital spending. The direction of the results is encouraging: operating cash flow exceeds capital spending throughout the displayed period, and the reported owner-earnings figures rise over the period.

## Interpretation

The mechanical screen is favorable. The combination of consistently strong reported ROE, positive owner earnings, and growth in owner earnings suggests a business with profitable operations and an ability to generate cash after capital spending.

These checks do not, by themselves, establish that Costco is attractively valued or that its competitive position will remain durable. A passing screen is a starting point for further research, not a complete investment decision.

## Limitations and missing information

The report does not find an R&D value. That does not mean Costco has no research and development spending; it means this particular data retrieval did not identify a value for the requested measure. The report also does not assess valuation, future growth, competitive threats, or the sustainability of margins and returns.

## Next research needed

Review Costco's latest annual report and relevant notes for the nature of its capital spending and any research-related expenses not captured by the selected XBRL tag. Then assess valuation, membership economics, competitive advantages, and risks to future cash generation before making an actual investment decision.
