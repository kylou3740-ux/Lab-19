# Costco — Cathie Wood Investor Memo

Verdict: PASS

## Screening results

Costco's reported annualized revenue growth from the first fiscal year to the last is 7.51%. That result fails the revenue-growth screen at the original 15% threshold. The printed report also states that latest-year research and development as a share of revenue is not found, so the R&D screen cannot be evaluated from the available data.

The separate threshold experiment lowered the revenue-growth requirement to 5%. Under that experimental assumption, Costco passes the revenue-growth check because its reported growth rate is above the revised threshold. The R&D result remains not known. The original threshold has been restored for the baseline analysis.

## Interpretation

Under the original screen, Costco does not meet the specified high-growth revenue criterion. The threshold experiment demonstrates how a different assumption changes the mechanical result, but it does not change Costco's reported financial history. Lowering a threshold should be justified by the investor's actual strategy rather than selected simply to produce a pass.

The unavailable R&D figure is an important limitation. It should not be interpreted as zero spending or as evidence that Costco fails the R&D test.

## Limitations and missing information

The printed report provides a historical revenue-growth measure, not a forecast of future growth. It does not establish whether Costco has the innovation profile, addressable-market opportunity, or future growth potential sought by a growth-oriented investor. The requested R&D measure was not found in the retrieved facts.

## Next research needed

Inspect Costco's annual-report disclosures for research-related expenses and determine whether a different disclosed measure is appropriate. Research the company's growth opportunities, digital and operational investments, membership model, and competitive environment. Retain the original screen unless there is a clearly reasoned basis for changing its threshold.
