# NVIDIA --- Cathie Wood Investor Memo

Verdict: OWN

## Screening results

NVIDIA passes both Cathie Wood checks in the printed SEC report.
Reported annualized revenue growth from the first fiscal year to the
last is 68.30%, above the screen's 15% threshold. Latest-year research
and development expense is reported as 8.6% of revenue, above the
required 5%.

## Interpretation

The mechanical screen is favorable. The reported revenue growth
indicates rapid expansion over the five-year period, while the R&D ratio
indicates that NVIDIA is committing a meaningful share of revenue to
research and development.

These results are consistent with a growth-oriented investment profile,
but they do not prove that the company's growth rate is sustainable. A
high historical growth rate can slow as the revenue base becomes larger,
and R&D spending does not by itself guarantee successful products or
attractive returns.

## Limitations and missing information

The revenue growth measure compares the first and last years in the
five-year report; it does not mean revenue grew at that rate in every
individual year. The R&D ratio is the latest-year figure reported by the
program. The screen does not measure the commercial success of
individual research projects, future product demand, or valuation.

## Next research needed

Investigate the sources and durability of NVIDIA's growth, including
demand from data-center customers, competition, product cycles, and the
economics of its software ecosystem. Review R&D priorities and whether
innovation is translating into durable competitive advantages. Compare
valuation with realistic forward-looking growth and profitability
assumptions before making an actual investment decision.
