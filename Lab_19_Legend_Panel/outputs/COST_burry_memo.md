# Costco — Michael Burry Investor Memo

Verdict: WATCH

## Screening results

Costco passes both Burry checks in the printed SEC report. Operating cash flow is at least as large as net income in every reported fiscal year. The latest reported liabilities-to-equity ratio is 1.49, which is below the screen's maximum of two times equity.

## Interpretation

The mechanical screen is favorable. Operating cash flow consistently supports reported earnings, while the latest liabilities-to-equity result remains within the specified limit. These findings are consistent with cash generation and a balance sheet that passes this particular leverage test.

The screen is not a full assessment of financial risk. A pass does not establish that the shares are undervalued, that liabilities are low relative to every relevant measure of cash flow, or that future results will remain stable. The report's current-ratio figures also vary across the period, so liquidity deserves separate review rather than being inferred from the liabilities-to-equity check alone.

## Limitations and missing information

The report is based on the selected SEC facts and XBRL tags. It does not provide a full maturity analysis of obligations, lease commitments, liquidity stress test, valuation analysis, or assessment of possible changes in operating conditions. R&D is not found in the report, although that is not one of the two Burry checks shown here.

## Next research needed

Review the annual-report notes for debt, lease obligations, commitments, and liquidity. Examine working-capital trends and cash conversion, then compare the company's valuation with its financial resilience and downside risks. Keep the company on a watchlist pending that deeper work.
